# Pitch Craft

## When to use
Need to explain a company, community, or offer to anyone — a prospect, partner, a DFY client, a webinar audience.

## The core formula
**[Who you help] + [Problem you solve] + [How you're different].** Everything else is decoration.

## Length variants
### The tweet (10s)
"We help [who] [do what] without [pain point]."

### The elevator (30s)
"[Tweet version]. Most [who] struggle with [problem] because [reason]. We [unique approach]."

### The coffee chat (2 min)
1. Hook: a surprising fact/question about the problem
2. Problem: "[Who] waste [time/money] on [problem] because [root cause]."
3. Solution: "We built [product] that [key benefit]. Unlike [alternatives], we [differentiator]."
4. Proof: [traction / social proof — compliant, no income claims]
5. Ask: "We're looking for [what you want]."

### The full pitch (5 min)
Add: team, market, business model, competition, roadmap.

## Process
1. Answer first: who specifically? their problem in their words? what you do (no jargon)? why different? what proof?
2. Test it out loud. If you stumble, simplify.

## Anti-patterns
- "We're a platform that…" (vague) · jargon ("revolutionary", "disrupt") · feature lists over benefits · no clear "who".
