# Meeting Prep

## When to use
A meeting soon and you need to walk in prepared — DFY discovery call, prospect, partner.

## What I need
1. **Who** are you meeting (name, company, role)?
2. **Context** (intro call, follow-up, DFY discovery, etc.)?
3. **What you want** from the meeting?

## Process
1. **Quick research (if time):** LinkedIn/background, recent company news, mutual connections, prior interactions (pull from GHL/notes).
2. **Generate the brief:**
```
## Meeting Brief: [Name] @ [Company]
Who they are: [role, background, context]
Why this meeting: [purpose]
Your goal: [what you want to walk away with]
3 things to mention: 1) [connection point] 2) [about their work] 3) [your hook]
Questions to ask: 1) [about their situation] 2) [advances your goal] 3) [relationship builder]
Watch out for: [sensitivities]
```
3. **One-liner opener:** "Hey [Name], thanks for making time. I saw [recent thing] — [comment]. Excited to [reason]."

## No time? Minimum viable prep
Know their name + company · why you're meeting · ONE clear ask · ONE good question.
