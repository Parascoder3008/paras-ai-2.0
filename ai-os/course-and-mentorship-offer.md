# Course plus long-term mentorship: offer specification

Updated with Paras's pricing direction on 2026-09-13. This is a reviewable offer specification, not a published checkout or approved contract.

## Confirmed direction

Bundle a structured course with ongoing mentorship. Indian annual option: **₹19,999 for the whole year**. Core audience: owners of small service businesses, with practical AI systems and the Content/Code/Capital/Collaboration framework. Primary 90-day target remains 200 active paying members. Free community members retain their existing free status unless they choose a paid offer.

India monthly base: **₹3,999/month**. A selected ₹500 discount yields **₹3,499/month**. ₹2,499/month was mentioned as a possible deeper discount, not selected as the launch price. The earlier “9 months / 12 months” payment-plan interpretation is withdrawn following Paras's clarification.

Annual is **₹19,999 paid once for 12 months**. At the monthly base, 12 payments total ₹47,988. The annual option is substantially cheaper: make its upfront commitment and exact access period clear. Do not invent extra benefits or a fake list price to justify that gap. No automatic renewal was authorized for annual buyers.

Future geographic proposals only: UAE/Middle East **AED 300/month**; US **$49 or $79/month**, not selected. Currency, local billing, support times, language and fulfilment must be decided before expansion. Do not convert currencies or launch global checkouts in this sprint.

## Recommended packaging for review

Working name: **AI Business Systems: Course + 12-Month Group Mentorship**.

Promise: learn a repeatable method to design, test and maintain useful AI-assisted business systems. Proposed first-month milestone: demonstrate one working system for a real task using sample or authorized data. Individual outcomes depend on participation, tool access and the task chosen; no income or client-acquisition guarantee.

Course map:
1. Task selection, baseline, clear inputs/outputs and human responsibility.
2. Business context, instructions, tools, reusable skills and evaluation.
3. Content workflow: real work → recording → native platform insights → a useful resource.
4. Code workflow: simple website/app or internal tool with testing and handoff.
5. Follow-up and operations: consent, CRM state, drafts, approval and failure recovery.
6. Collaboration: roles, documentation, peer feedback, service delivery and scope.
7. Capital: track costs, cash collections and time; judge whether a system is worth maintaining.
8. Capstone: demonstrate, explain limitations, share a reusable template and maintenance checklist.

Long-term mentorship proposal: one guided implementation session and one group office hour per week; one monthly implementation theme; recordings; community accountability; Sanchit-managed question triage. Confirm this capacity before sale. No unlimited private consulting, custom builds or guaranteed individual review on demand. Early pilot of 15–25 members is a capacity test, not a cap on the 90-day ambition.

Free layer: starter resource, one introductory course/lesson and useful public demonstrations. Paid layer: structured progression, implementation support and sustained feedback. The community is not a dumping ground for unrelated promotions.

## Revenue treatment

200 × ₹19,999 = **₹39,99,800 annual sales value**, if all 200 choose and pay for that annual offer. Dividing by 12 yields **₹3,33,316.67 monthly value equivalent**. Neither is verified current revenue. Annual entitlement does not imply an automatically renewing subscription.

Track separate fields: annual upfront cash, monthly payment collections, unpaid instalments, refunds, paid access start/end, renewal election, cancellations, active entitled members and recognized revenue under the accounting policy. Do not count the entire annual payment as recurring monthly MRR. 200 monthly members at ₹3,999 would produce ₹7,99,800 gross monthly billing, an alternative scenario to 200 annual buyers. A mixed cohort must be calculated by billing plan. Monthly budget remains ₹2 lakh; protect cash for the future support obligation.
