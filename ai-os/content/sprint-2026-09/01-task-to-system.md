## LinkedIn insight draft

Before assigning a task to AI, I write down its input, expected output and reviewer. “Handle customer inquiries” hides several different jobs. “Prepare a reply using approved information, then wait for review” is something I can test. For service-business owners, that task boundary is often the most useful starting point. Pick one recurring task and write down what must happen when information is missing. The worksheet accompanying this week's demonstration follows that structure.
