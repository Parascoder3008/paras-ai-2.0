## Demonstration to rehearse

1. Open the actual raw recording and its transcript.
2. Pull **2–3 standalone short ideas** — moments that stand on their own without the long-form context. Show one candidate that looked good in the timeline and fails standalone, and say why it fails.
3. Write the LinkedIn insight from the *idea*, not from the transcript. Show the transcript-paste version side by side and let the viewer see why it reads worse. This is short-12's whole point.
4. Draft the X thread as five claims, each survivable alone.
5. Show the resource brief that ties all of it to one destination — one link, not five.
6. Show the production board row updating: package, owner, status. Not a fake "scheduled" state — the real `briefed` value, with the publication gate still closed.

## Claims discipline

No view counts, no reach numbers, no "yeh format viral gaya". Our own channel figures are a dated snapshot in `../../metrics/baseline.json`, and a snapshot is not a performance claim. Judge the work by whether it produced a qualified opt-in, and say out loud that we do not have that measurement yet.

Do not present the Instagram five-destination problem as fixed. `CURRENT-PLAN.md` records that no public link or bio has been changed. Frame it as the decision being made, not a completed cleanup.
