# Fourteen daily shorts: Sep 14–27, 2026

One short per day is the baseline. The second is optional and only from an approved buffer — `CURRENT-PLAN.md` is explicit that duplicate cuts published to hit a count are not wanted.

Owner Devan; coordinator Redima; factual approval Paras. IDs match `../production-board.json` (`short-01` … `short-14`). Hinglish spoken, English tool names, readable captions.

**These are drafts.** Days 1–6 and 8–10 are derived from the OS01/OS02/OS03 material and must be re-cut against what the long-form recording actually shows. Days 7, 11 and 14 depend on real events in the week and cannot be pre-written truthfully — their briefs say what to capture, not what to say.

## Standing rules for every short

One idea. Visible payoff in the first second — the screen or the claim, not a face saying "aaj hum baat karenge". No background music requirement; original narration is the default. Any music, stock or template needs its licence recorded by Devan before publication.

No income claims, no "X din mein result", no invented client outcomes, no time-saved figure that was not measured on camera. Synthetic data only; blur credentials, contacts and stray browser tabs. Never show `.env`, `.mcp.json` or a contact CSV.

CTA rule: until the resource is hosted and its delivery tested from a logged-out phone, shorts carry **no link CTA**. They end on the idea. `resource-delivery` is an open blocker owned by Redima.

## Handoff

Devan builds the buffer ahead of the date, not on the date. Redima checks each short against the claims rules above and against the long-form it came from before it reaches Paras. Paras approves the factual content and the cut. Nothing here is scheduled or published until the board's publication gate passes — `node scripts/ops.cjs check` will list a short as publish-ready only when evidence, rights, approval, tested CTA and final asset are all recorded.
