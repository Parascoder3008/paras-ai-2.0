## 1. Start Here post — draft

> **Welcome. Padhne se pehle ek baat.**
>
> Yeh community un service-business owners ke liye hai jo apne business ke ek repeat hone wale kaam ko AI ki madad se system bana rahe hain. Sirf tools dekhne ke liye nahi — apna pehla working system banane ke liye.
>
> **Yahan kya hota hai**
> Har hafte ek real demonstration. Har mahine ek implementation sprint. Members apna kaam dikhate hain, doubts poochhte hain, aur jo fail hua woh bhi share karte hain.
>
> **Aapka pehla kaam — aaj, dus minute**
> Apne business ka ek recurring task choose karo. Uska input, expected output, reviewer, aur failure case likho. Starter kit mein worksheet hai. Bas ek task. Poora business nahi.
>
> **Yahan kya nahi milega**
> Guaranteed income ya returns ka koi promise nahi. Koi trading tips ya buy/sell signals nahi. Aapke business ka kaam aapko hi karna hai; yahan structure aur feedback milta hai.
>
> **Do cheezein**
> Introduce yourself: business kya hai, aur kaunsa ek kaam aapka sabse zyada time leta hai. Aur jab kuch build karo, result dikhao — jo weak nikla woh bhi. Failure share karna yahan normal hai.

Post only after the space is live and the starter kit link passes its delivery test.

## 2. Community rules — draft

Six, so they can be read.

1. **Ek business, ek problem.** Post karo toh apna actual kaam aur actual blocker likho. "AI kaise seekhein" se kisi ki help nahi hoti.
2. **Koi income ya returns ka claim nahi.** Apna ya kisi aur ka. Yeh education space hai. Trading tips, buy/sell calls aur profit screenshots allowed nahi hain.
3. **Promotion sirf allowed thread mein.** Apni service ke links bina permission ke post mat karo. Members ko DM karke sell karna ban hai.
4. **Doosron ka data mat post karo.** Screenshots se customer names, numbers, emails aur credentials hata do. Synthetic data use karo.
5. **Jo fail hua woh share karo.** Pura kaam dikhao — jo chala aur jo nahi chala. Yahi sabse useful posts hain.
6. **Jo unknown hai usko unknown likho.** Yahan bhi wahi rule jo systems mein hai.

Breaking these: first time an edit request, second time removal of the post, repeated or commercial spam removal from the group. Sanchit applies it consistently and logs each action.

## 3. Welcome message — draft

Short, one question, no pitch. Send only through an approved channel, only to people who joined, and only after Paras approves this exact text.

> Welcome. Do sawaal: aapka business kya hai, aur kaunsa ek kaam har hafte repeat hota hai aur sabse zyada time leta hai?
>
> Jo aap batayenge, usi se main aapko relevant starting point bhej paunga. Start Here post mein pehla task diya hai — dus minute ka hai.

No membership invitation in the welcome. The invitation belongs after the person has done something, and it is a separate approved message.

## 4. First-task tracking

The free community's job is to produce people who have finished one small thing. That is the honest signal of who is ready for the paid membership, and it is far more reliable than who watched a session.

Sanchit records per member: joined date, business type, the recurring task they named, whether the worksheet came back, and whether they demonstrated anything. Keep names and contact details in GHL, not in the public space and not in git.

Lifecycle, matching `../../automation/README.md`: `resource_requested` → `resource_delivered` → `session_registered` → `session_attended` / `no_show` → `offer_interested`. A member does not become `offer_interested` because they were active. They become it when they say so.

## 5. Moderation queue — open, blocker `moderation`

Observed 2026-09-13 and not yet actioned:

- **Two reported posts.** Read both, decide against the rules above, log the decision and tell the reporter what happened.
- **Four pending requests.** Approve or decline each; do not leave them open. A four-month-old ignored request is a member lost quietly.
- **Visible promotional posts.** Remove or relocate to an allowed thread. Do these before the Start Here post goes up — publishing rules over an unmoderated feed teaches members the rules are decorative.

Order of work: moderate → post rules → post Start Here → pin → then any welcome messages.

## 6. Invitation into the paid membership — hold

Drafting is fine; sending is not, and the blockers are real ones, not paperwork. Support scope is undefined, the checkout path is unverified, and renewal and refund handling are undecided. Selling a 12-month commitment before those are settled creates an obligation the delivery side has not agreed to.

When it is ready, the invitation is transparent and unpressured: what the membership is, what the first 30 days produce, the confirmed price, what is explicitly not included, and how to leave. No countdown timers, no invented scarcity, no income framing. The Thursday public demonstration is the natural place for it — a genuine invitation at the end of something genuinely useful.

Until then the correct answer to "kya paid bhi hai?" is a plain one: yes, it is being finalised, and here is the confirmed price, and it opens when the access and support side is ready.
