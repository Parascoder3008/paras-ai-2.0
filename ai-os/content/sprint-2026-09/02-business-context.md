## LinkedIn insight draft

A business-context file should tell an AI assistant what is current, what is approved and what is unknown. I keep the current plan separate from archived decisions and give each handoff an owner. Otherwise a well-written response can still use an old offer or send work to nobody. The useful test is simple: ask a question whose answer is missing. Does the workflow report the gap, or manufacture certainty?
