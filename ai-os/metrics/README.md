# Weekly measurement contract

Baseline: `baseline.json`, observed 2026-09-13. Unknown values are null, never zero. Social figures are a dated snapshot, not a live integration. Refresh manually from authenticated sources until the read-only sync is implemented and tested.

## Revenue and membership

- **Active paid member:** unique person with verified paid course/mentorship access covering the measurement date. Exclude free members and uncollected orders. Payment failures and any grace period must follow an agreed policy.
- **Annual cash collected:** settled payments for the ₹19,999 one-time 12-month plan, minus relevant refunds; cash date matters.
- **Monthly collections:** actual settled ₹3,999 or approved discounted monthly payments. Do not treat potential future payments as collected cash.
- **Annual monthly value equivalent:** annual access price divided by 12, reported separately. It is not automatically renewing monthly MRR.
- **Recurring subscription MRR:** use only when the provider confirms an active recurring subscription and its billing terms. Record actual plan price, discounts and cancellation status.
- **Active members roll-forward:** opening active + new paid + reactivated - expired access - cancellations ending access = closing active, with an explanation for corrections. Deduplicate annual/monthly transitions.
- **Paid retention:** retained eligible members divided by the cohort eligible to renew. Annual access and monthly renewals have different denominators.
- **First result:** member demonstrates a tested workflow against the agreed rubric. Attendance, watching a lesson or downloading a template is not completion.

## Funnel

Track source, content ID, resource ID and cohort in GHL. Qualified opt-in means an identified person who selected the relevant business interest and agreed to the applicable contact purpose/channel. Track resource delivery, registrations, unique attendees, offer interest, new paid and first result separately. One person attending three sessions is not three prospects.

Weekly conversions: registration/qualified opt-in; attendance/registration; new paid/eligible unique attendees; first result/new paid cohort. Use the same acquisition cohort/window or state the mismatch. Payment can happen after the event week. Never divide followers by members and call it conversion.

## Content

YouTube: long-form views, impressions, CTR, average view duration, first-30-second retention and subscribers by comparable content/traffic source. Shorts: engaged views, stayed-to-watch, average percentage watched, profile/related-video actions where available. Instagram: non-follower reach, watch time, saves/shares, profile actions and tracked resource opt-ins. Public grid views do not expose all these metrics.

Report two views: audience performance and qualified business demand. A viral consumer-AI reel can win the first and fail the second. No automatic 10x-only rule; use multiple comparable uploads, sufficient impressions and acquisition quality. Treat experiments with small samples as inconclusive.
